Open VoiceBean

Tap “Connect my device” and choose soundcore Work. The first time, you'll be asked for Bluetooth access. Tap OK.

  1. Install the free Bluefy browser Safari on iPhone can't use Bluetooth, but Bluefy can. Come back to this page after installing. Get Bluefy on the App Store
  2. Open VoiceBean in Bluefy This button switches to Bluefy and opens it there. Open in Bluefy
  3. Connect your recorder In Bluefy, tap “Connect my device”. When asked for Bluetooth access, tap OK, then choose soundcore Work.
  4. (Optional) Put an icon on your Home Screen On this Safari page, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. The icon opens VoiceBean in Bluefy.
