  1. Install the free Bluefy browser Safari on iPhone can't use Bluetooth, but Bluefy can. Come back to this page after installing. Get Bluefy on the App Store
  2. Open VoiceBean in Bluefy This button switches to Bluefy and opens it there. Open in Bluefy
  3. Connect your recorder In Bluefy, tap “Connect my device”. When asked for Bluetooth access, tap OK, then choose soundcore Work. Once connected, open Recordings to download and play.
  4. (Optional) Put an icon on your Home Screen On this Safari page, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. The icon opens VoiceBean in Bluefy.

Please use a different browser

This browser can't use Bluetooth. On a computer or Android phone, open the link below in Chrome or Edge. On iPhone, use Bluefy.

Tips

  • Take the recorder out of its case, or open the lid and press its button to wake it.
  • The recorder connects to one phone at a time. Tap Disconnect when you're done so the next person can connect.
  • Keep the page open while downloading; switching away drops Bluetooth.
  • Recordings stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
